About the portal

Welcome to the Job Prediction Service portal!

This portal can be used by comapnies and students as well. Company and specially HR Professionals can use this particular web application to plan the recruitment according to current job market. Companies lose great talent if they dont' keep the salary or benefits as per the market. This portal will give you guys an insight on the current market conditions and will help you to plan your hiring expectations accordingly.

This portal can be used by students to check the availability of remote jobs and work type offered by the market based on their expectations. You guys as well can check the current market conditions in the following visualizations.

Click here to check the current market conditions based on our dataset.

Company Portal

In this portal you will be able to perform the following tasks:

  1. Find out the maximum salary offered based on current market conditions. This prediction will be based on the minimum salary offered by the company, work type, remote jobs, sponsorship offered, experience level needed for the job, and type of application needed.

Student Portal

In this portal you will be able to perform the following tasks:

  1. You will be able to predict if the job can be remote or not based on the minimum salary you are expecting, type of work you want, if you want sponsorship or not, experience level needed for the job you are looking for, and type of application.
  2. You will be able to predict minimum salary expectations, based on if the job is remote or not, if you want sponsorship or not, experience level you are looking for, and type of application.
